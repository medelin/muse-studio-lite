# Policy checklist (cite rule ids in every finding)

- **P1 Personal attributes** — never assert or imply a person's attributes (health, age, finances, religion, ethnicity, ...): "Are you lonely?" is a violation.
- **P2 Before/after and body image** — no before/after comparisons, no idealised bodies.
- **P3 Unrealistic promises** — no guaranteed results, no exaggerated or unverifiable claims.
- **P4 Price and offer accuracy** — prices, discounts and delivery terms must match the brand sheet exactly; no invented offers.
- **P5 Brand name** — the brand appears with its full name; no misleading affiliation with other companies or Meta.
- **P6 Image text** — at most the headline as text in the image; no fake buttons, no fake UI.
- **P7 Prohibited or sensitive content** — no weapons, drugs, adult content, shocking imagery, faces of real people without consent.
- **P8 Regulated claims** — no medical, legal, financial or tax claims or advice.
- **P9 Official services and absolute tax/legal promises** — the advertiser must not claim to register companies or to provide government documents or official services ("register your company", "załóż spółkę", "we file your official registration"), and must not promise absolute tax or legal outcomes ("no ZUS obligation", "pay no tax", "100% legal"). Google Ads policies: "Government documents and official services" and "Exaggerated or inaccurate claims". Severity: block.
